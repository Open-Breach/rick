What the frame should settle

  • Height and padding: as the toolbar, button/size + 2 × space/toolbar-y, space/toolbar-x padding; 4 px track as the other progress and usage bars.
  • Past state: bg/selected-soft band with text/on-selected, a Read-only pill and a secondary Back to now button in place of Now. The file list stays as it is; the breadcrumb shows the snapshot as its own segment (@weekly-45).
  • Label: date and time, then the point's label and source; at Now, "Now" and the number of earlier states.
  • Ticks: 2 × 12 px; ticks closer than 3 px drawn as one wider tick; the handle a 14 px accent/focus disc.
  • Reading: label and an empty track only, no ticks; the bar goes away again if the answer is empty.
  • Focus: a 2 px accent/focus ring inside the bar; the tooltip is a card with date, time, label and source.
  • Open: whether the past band should use a scheme token of its own rather than bg/selected-soft, which also marks selected rows.
